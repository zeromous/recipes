# "Hot-N-Sour" Soup

Makes 24 Servings (2cup)

## Measurements / Shopping List
Approximate Cost $40USD ($55 Cdn)

> All measurements are exact, use measuring spoons, and adjust to taste at each step of the broth.

>⚠️Do not add pepper early or in excessive amounts.  Adjust Vinegar and slurry to taste/tang+spice/thickness  

### Proteins and Veggies
- 1 pkg Shitake Mushrooms (Dried, at least 24 good sized)
- 1 pkg Black Mushrooms (Dried ~ 2cup (1 cup when dry))
- 1 pkg dried Lily Buds (1 Cup)
- 1 large can (~400=500g) Bamboo Shoots (sliced)
- 1 pkg Firm Tofu (~ 1lb)
- ~1 lb porkloin

### Soup Base
- 1-1 1/3 cups Chicken Stock (Plain, no tumeric)
- 1 Soy Sauce Dark (8-12tbsp) 
- 1 Soy Sauce Light (4tbsp)
- 2-2.5 tbsp Sesame Oil ( ~1 per 16 cups)

### Finishes
- 1/4 to 1/2 cup Chinkiang (Plum) Vinegar
- Cornstarch (1-1 1/3 cup) + ~2 cups of Ice-Cold water
- White Pepper (2-2.5 teasspoons)
- 2 to 3 large eggs
- Bunch of Green Onion (Garnish)

## Initial Prep

- Soak Shitake in airtight bag (24) once hydrated (approx 3h)
- Soak Black Mushroom in bowl (2Cups Dry) and rinse thoroughly once hydrated (approx 1h)
- Soak Lily Buds (1Cup) remove from water once hydrated and trim bases of lily bud.

### Stock

40 Cup Cold water
- Add Chicken stock to water and heat to boil.

### Prepare Proteins (While stock waiting to boil)

- ~1 lb pork loin - Lightly freeze while preparing mushrooms (<1h)
- Pare stems and slice mushrooms into 1/8 inch strips(this takes like an hour!).  

  > ⚠️Be sure to remove entire stem and be careful with mushrooms that are not fully hydrated or slippery!  
  
- Cut lightly frozen Pork into to 1/8inch slices, trimming excess fat leave a little on there!)
- Cut Tofu length-wise and width-wise in to to ~1 x 1/8 x 1/8 inch strips   
  
🥣🥣🥣 Taste the broth and ensure a light chicken flavor

### Boiling Stock
- Soy Sauce Dark up to 5-6 tbsp (for color)
- Soy Sauce Light up to 2-2.5 tbsp (for salt) to taste (and consider how strong salty chicken broth already is)
  
🥣🥣🥣 Taste the broth to sure a light caramel color and enjoyably salty taste
 
### Adding Proteins and Vegetables
- Add Pork and stir until lightly cooked (~15s)
- Add mushrooms
- Add Bamboo Shoots 
- Add Lily Buds
- Add Tofu
- Stir and Bring back to rapid boil, covered
  
### Adding Aeromatics
- (When Boiling) Add 3-4 tbsp of Sriacha and Garlic and stir
- (When Boiling) Add 2-2.5 tbsp Sesame sauce (careful, err on side of less it is strong flavor!), stir and cover
   
🥣🥣🥣 Taste the broth to ensure a very light chilli with garlic and pleasant salty flavor 

### Prepare for finishing
- Seperately Prepare Slurry (cornstarch + ICE cold water) for finishing, well whisked
- Add 2-2.5 teaspoons (up to 3 for extra heat) pepper to slurry and whisk again.  It should be a light cappucino color using white pepper.
- Beat Eggs

### Finishing  (15m before serving)
- Turn down boil to low-med heat

> ⚠️We want to let the soup settle from a boil before we add the vinegar, pepper and slurry.
    
> ⚠️Ensure slurry is smooth and continue to whisk as necessary.  Ice bath can help keep it cool if you prepared it earlier.   

- Add Chinkiang Vinegar (1/3 to 1/2 cup to taste), stir

🥣🥣🥣 Taste the broth to ensure a mushroomy, sour taste and peppery hit.
  
- Slowly stir in Pepper slurry, stopping half way to test pepper

🥣🥣🥣 Taste the broth to ensure a sour taste and building heat.  Y
🥣🥣🥣 You do not want to taste pepper, only heat.  If it is not sour enough, try adding a little bit more vinegar (1-2 tablespoons

- Slowly stir in rest of pepper-slurry until you are satisfied with the heat-level
- Continue on low-med heat, stirring occaisionally for 10m to allow for thickening.
- (Optional - To Thicken more): You can add more plain slurry (without pepper) at this time (for a slightly thicker soup) and continue to cook on low-medium heat a bit longer 

### Just before serving Fresh:
- Egg Drop (get someone to help)
  - consistely stir soup to ensure circular motion
  - gently drop egg out of the bowl using a fork beating motion to string the egg in to the soup while the soup spins
 
- Remove from Heat
- Chop fresh green onions for garnish and **serve**

Cool overnight, then freeze leftover servings and reheat/slowcook within 3 months!  Perfect snack a winter snow shovel, or ski day, or whenever you just feel rundown.
